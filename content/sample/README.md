# Sample content

The project M0 runs everywhere (M0 W8, AB-T-0509): the cafe, its doors, Mira (the player) and Hana (an NPC), an
animation set, a recorded demo walk. Asset refs in the scenes are paths under this folder, so it opens as a project in
the playground, the MCP server (`node engine/packages/mcp/src/server.js content/sample cafe.vrmxs --behaviours
behaviours.js --vrmxa-core ../../../vrmxa-avatar-studio/packages/vrmxa-core`) and the engine's tests.

**Every file has a licence: [LICENCES.md](LICENCES.md)**, checked by `tools/check-licences.mjs` in CI.

| M3 W12 clips | Gestures for conversations: `shrug`, `point` and `laugh` (masked one-shots), `wave` (the greet clip under its own slot), and `getUp`, a full-body action from kneeling to standing that a ragdoll's get-up blends into |

| `behaviours.js` | The project's `wander` |
| `takes/conversation.json` | **A conversation take** (M3 W4): two minutes of Mira walking in and talking with Hana (the host brain), with the director's split turn on a scripted model; the scene, the cast, the input (`input/walk-in.json`) and the ai log. | `takes/follow.json` | **A follower take** (M3 W12): 70 s in `cafe-follow.vrmxs`; Mira walks in, asks Hana to wait by the door, talks about the weather (Hana shrugs), calls her along (she follows) and teases her (she laughs); a scripted model, made by `tools/make-follow-take.mjs`. Every take in `takes/` replays identically twice in the director's fixture test |
| `sequences/conversation.vrmxs` | That take baked into a sequence (`takeToSequence`): Hana's six lines and Mira's six as dialogue clips (baked to audio with `tools/bake-lines.mjs`, af_heart and af_sky), a decisions track, an input track, one shot |
| `sequences/morning.vrmxs` | The M2 W1 sequence: 12 s, four shots of Mira arriving and Hana welcoming her |
| `sequences/cafe-morning.vrmxs` | **"Morning at the cafe"** (M2 W11): 45 s, five shots (Morning, Arrival, The order, Steam, Served), four baked lines, a music cue on the arrival, the espresso steam, the `looks/morning.cube` grade. | `scripts/cafe-order.fountain`, `sequences/cafe-order.vrmxs` | **"The Order"** (M4 W4): Mira orders at the counter and Hana serves her, as a script in the Fountain subset, and its sequence built by `@vrmxg/script` (`node content/sample/tools/make-script-sequence.mjs cafe-order`; marks in `scripts/cafe-marks.json`): 11 shots, seven lines voiced by `tools/bake-lines.mjs --fit` (M4 W7: Hana `af_heart`, Mira `af_bella`; each line's clip covers its speech, and a re-script keeps the bake and the lengths), talk, gestures, faces, look-at and default cameras |
| `scripts/the-regular.fountain`, `sequences/the-regular.vrmxs`, `shorts.json` | **"The Regular"** (M4 W12, AB-T-0584), **the M4 sample short**: one minute (61.4 s) of Mira, a regular, telling Hana she got the job across town, then taking her latte to the window table. Built and kept up by one tool per step, each re-runnable and changing nothing once done: `tools/make-script-sequence.mjs` (every short in `shorts.json`, with its cameras: this one by rule, `@vrmxg/coverage`, 18 shots), `tools/cast-short.mjs` (`@vrmxg/cast`: Hana and Mira from `characters/`, voices `af_heart` and `af_bella` into `sequence.roles`), `tools/bake-lines.mjs sequences/the-regular.vrmxs --fit` (13 lines in Kokoro, no overlaps; `--fit` re-scripts with the short's own marks and cameras), and `tools/make-short-take.mjs` (a hand-keyed performance take: gestures and faces placed against lines and actions, items with no `src`, so a re-script keeps them; re-run it after a re-script that changes a beat's length). `tools/replay-short.mjs` plays it in Node twice, in two processes: same final hash, every line spoken from its track, a mid-line snapshot restored exactly (`acceptance/results/m4-short-replay.json`). `shorts.json` lists the creator's shorts: The Order (the script's default cameras) and The Regular (by rule) |
| `park.vrmxs`, `sets/park.glb`, `props/{bench,street-lamp,planter-tree,vending-machine}.glb` | **The park** (M4 W12), the sample's second set, **made with Gaea**: a 10 m paved square beside the cafe, hedges on three sides, lawns, and four props from the local Gaea (`tools/generate-park.mjs`: Z-Image-Turbo pictures, then TRELLIS.2 meshes scaled to each prop's real size: **"Built with DINOv3"**). `tools/make-park.mjs` writes the rest: the ground, hedges and an invisible box collider per prop (`sets/park.glb`), the set scene with Mira and Hana (validated), and `scripts/park-marks.json`, which the creator picks up for any script whose `Set:` is `park.vrmxs` |
| `brains/` | Behaviour trees as `vrmxg-brain` documents: Hana serving, a customer |
| `effects/espresso-steam.json` | The steam wand: a dense jet, then wisps |
| `looks/morning.cube` | A 17³ LUT: cool lifted shadows, warm highlights (`tools/make-lut.mjs`) |
| `voice/` | Lines (Kokoro-82M through mlx-audio) with word timings. `voice/lines.json` lists M1–M3's (`tools/make-voice.py`). `tools/bake-lines.mjs <scene>` voices a sequence's dialogue into its track (M4 W7, `@vrmxg/voicing`: clips named by line, engine, voice and words; never written over) and a scene's text-only `say` connections, without touching `lines.json`; `tools/tts-server.py` speaks lines live while playing (the playground's `?tts=http://127.0.0.1:5190/speak`); `tools/voice-server.py` (M3 W3) adds the director's model and speech-to-text on one port (5191): `/v1/chat/completions`, `/transcribe`, `/speak` |

Regenerate with `node content/sample/tools/make-content.mjs` (needs vrmxa-avatar-studio beside the repo for the
characters and clips) and `node content/sample/tools/make-input.mjs`; the prop with `tools/generate-prop.mjs` (needs
Gaea running). Voice lines: `HF_HUB_CACHE=<models> HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 <python with mlx-audio> -I tools/make-voice.py`
(only lines without a clip; `--all` remakes them).
