# Sample content

The project M0 runs everywhere (M0 W8, AB-T-0509): the cafe, its doors, Mira (the player) and Hana (an NPC), an
animation set, a recorded demo walk. Asset refs in the scenes are paths under this folder, so it opens as a project in
the playground, the MCP server (`node engine/packages/mcp/src/server.js content/sample cafe.vrmxs --behaviours
behaviours.js --vrmxa-core ../../../vrmxa-avatar-studio/packages/vrmxa-core`) and the engine's tests.

**Every file has a licence: [LICENCES.md](LICENCES.md)**, checked by `tools/check-licences.mjs` in CI.

| | |
|---|---|
| `cafe.vrmxs` | A 12 m room with a counter, tables and stools, the set as one GLB with KHR_physics colliders; the double entrance door (swings open as you walk into its trigger), a locked staff door; sun and counter spot light; an Environment; Mira outside on the pavement; Hana wandering; a follow camera |
| `prefabs/` | The door (frame, hinged panel, trigger) and the double door |
| `sets/`, `props/` | GLBs: generated by `tools/make-content.mjs` (boxes with flat colours), except the espresso machine, generated with the local Gaea (Z-Image-Turbo, then TRELLIS.2: **"Built with DINOv3"**) by `tools/generate-prop.mjs` |
| `characters/` | vrmxa-core's fixture bundles: Spec, as "Mira" (with a closet and a voice) and "Minimal" |
| `animations.json`, `animations/clips/` | CC0 clips generated by our own code: idle, walk, run, jump, fall, land, an idle with a sword, the item actions, and masked gestures (greet; the M2 W11 emotes nod, cheer and sip) |
| `input/walk-in.json` | 10 s of recorded input: walk in, run to the counter, look around, walk back, jump |
| `behaviours.js` | The project's `wander` |
| `sequences/morning.vrmxs` | The M2 W1 sequence: 12 s, four shots of Mira arriving and Hana welcoming her |
| `sequences/cafe-morning.vrmxs` | **"Morning at the cafe"** (M2 W11): 45 s, five shots (Morning, Arrival, The order, Steam, Served), four baked lines, a music cue on the arrival, the espresso steam, the `looks/morning.cube` grade. Hana and Mira are cast (`sequence.cast`) with the brains below, and the script is cut to what they do (`engine/packages/mcp/tools/beats.mjs`) |
| `brains/` | Behaviour trees as `vrmxg-brain` documents: Hana serving, a customer |
| `effects/espresso-steam.json` | The steam wand: a dense jet, then wisps |
| `looks/morning.cube` | A 17³ LUT: cool lifted shadows, warm highlights (`tools/make-lut.mjs`) |
| `voice/` | Lines (Kokoro-82M through mlx-audio) with word timings; `voice/lines.json` lists them. `tools/bake-lines.mjs <sequence>` bakes a sequence's text-only dialogue; `tools/tts-server.py` speaks lines live while playing (the playground's `?tts=http://127.0.0.1:5190/speak`) |

Regenerate with `node content/sample/tools/make-content.mjs` (needs vrmxa-avatar-studio beside the repo for the
characters and clips) and `node content/sample/tools/make-input.mjs`; the prop with `tools/generate-prop.mjs` (needs
Gaea running). Voice lines: `HF_HUB_CACHE=<models> HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 <python with mlx-audio> -I tools/make-voice.py`
(only lines without a clip; `--all` remakes them).
